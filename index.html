EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A next-generation technology and knowledge-transfer enterprise.

Spark Technologies Sdn Bhd (SPARK — Sabah AI Program for Robotics & Knowledge) is a next-generation technology and knowledge-transfer enterprise purpose-built to accelerate the digital economy transformation of Sabah, the wider Borneo region, and Malaysia at large.

Vision

To be the region's foremost catalyst for AI, robotics, and advanced knowledge transfer — empowering government, industry, and enterprise to leapfrog into a globally competitive, technology-driven future.

Mission

To deliver bespoke, high-specification technology solutions and internationally certified upskilling through an agile delivery model and a globally backed strategic ecosystem — aligned to Malaysia's national digital agenda and Sabah's regional priorities.
